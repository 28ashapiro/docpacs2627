CORE CHECKS -----
Timer begins at 20 seconds: ✅
Decreases once per ~second: ✅
Stops at zero: ✅
Never becomes a negative: ✅
Direction changes about every 2 seconds: ✅
Only UP, DOWN, LEFT, RIGHT appear: ✅
Direction changing stops when the game ends: ✅
Restarting restores timer and game state: ✅

SYSTEM CHECKS -----
Left scoring direction awards a point for pressing A and angled to the left: ✅
Right scoring direction awards a point for pressing A and angled to the right: ✅
Down scoring direction awards a point for pressing A and angled up: ✅
Up scoring direction awards a point for pressing A and angled down: ✅
All scoring directions do not award points for facing any direction but the scoring direction: ✅
Multiple points can be earned by fulfilling the same conditions and pressing A multiple times: ✅
Strong diagonals DO NOT count: ✅
Generally fails safely: ✅
